The OnChainFest ecosystem

Play it. Prove it. Own the Bragging Rights.

OnChainFest is the tournament platform. Bragging Rights ($BRT) is an NFT layer within the broader ecosystem — being developed to turn verified sporting achievements into digital recognition athletes can carry beyond a single event.

$BRT is not what powers the tournament. It extends the experience: the event creates the result, OnChainFest preserves the record, and Bragging Rights can give selected achievements a collectible digital expression.
